### Getting Started with MongoDB Modeler

**Step 1: Access the tool**
- Navigate to https://mongomodeler.com/editor.html
- No account or login required
- Works directly in your browser

**Step 2: Create a new collection**
- Click "Add Collection" button
- Name your collection (e.g., "users")
- The collection appears as a box in the workspace

**Step 3: Add fields to your collection**
- Click on the collection box
- Use the fields panel to add properties
- Specify field name, data type, and validation rules
