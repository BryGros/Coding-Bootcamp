# Welcome to Code-Cracker!

Created by Bryan Grosbach

## How to Play (Rules)

You’re a **passcode cracker** racing against a **firewall trace**. Your mission: **uncover as many passcodes as possible before time runs out**!

### 1) Choose your difficulty

- Your difficulty determines how long you have before the trace completes:
  - **60 seconds** or **90 seconds** (depending on difficulty)
- When the timer hits **0**, the firewall trace “kicks you out” and your run is evaluated.

### 2) Crack passcodes by making words

- You’re given **7 letters**.
- To uncover a passcode, you must submit a **valid word** built from those letters.

### 3) Word rules

- Words must be **at least 4 letters long**
- You **cannot reuse letters** in a word
  - If a letter appears once in the 7-letter set, you can only use it **once** in your submitted word

### 4) Scoring & bonuses

- Each uncovered passcode (valid word) awards points.
- **Longer words = more points**
- If you use **all 7 letters** in a single word, you earn a **decent “full-set” bonus**

### 5) End of timer: continue or game over

When time runs out, one of two things happens:

- **If you’ve earned enough points:** you can **keep playing** to increase your **total score**
- **If you haven’t earned enough points:** your run ends immediately

### 6) Logging your score & leaderboard

- Your game ends when you **end your code-cracking run** _or_ you **fail to meet the minimum score requirement** after the timer expires.
- When your run ends, your score is **logged**.
- If your score places you in the **Top 10**, you’ll appear on the **Leaderboard**.
